# A quick guide to getting started

Each teaching route, Fraction Peaks, arithmetic trail, and story activity opens with a **four-step visual tutorial**. The small animated example is separate from the live game and is not saved as a learner attempt.

Use **Next step**, **Back**, or **Replay** to explore the demonstration. Finish with **I’m ready**, or choose **Skip for now**. **Show me how** reopens it during the activity. Reduced-motion settings replace moving demonstrations and pulsing beacons with steady visual cues; the written steps remain available.

## Teach Pip in four steps

1. **Choose your teaching.** Select a method, set its fraction or amount, and press **Use this teaching**.
2. **Predict a fair share.** Open **Choose your prediction** and pick how many berries each friend should receive. **I’m not sure yet** is available. This prediction does not change Pip’s instruction.
3. **Let Pip try.** Press **Let Pip try this** and watch the bowls fill. Pause or resume when useful.
4. **Compare and revise.** To continue, everyone needs the same positive share and the basket must be empty. If the plan does not work here, choose **Try another idea**, change the instruction, and confirm it again before predicting and running.

The visible guide follows these actions. In Fraction Peaks it explains the arrows and beacon; in arithmetic and stories it explains entering, checking, and continuing after an answer.

## A nudge toward the next control

After **ten seconds without interaction**, a gentle light highlights the next relevant control. **Show me** brings it into view and focuses it; it never presses the control or supplies an answer. If that first hint is dismissed or the learner moves on, a second hint waits for **thirty quiet seconds**. After two hints, automatic guidance stays off for that activity during this page visit. **Show me how** remains available.

Interaction resets the quiet interval, not the two-hint budget. Changing a teaching, moving to another question or stage, and opening a dialog do not replenish hints. A new expedition, fraction climb, arithmetic round, or story gets a fresh budget. Guidance waits while a tutorial or another dialog is open, while Pip is executing a teaching, and while the relevant control is unavailable. Fraction session checks highlight the whole choices group, never the correct fraction.

## What is recorded

Tutorials and navigation beacons are control guidance. The teaching journal’s support count records **equal-share pictures and coach questions**; original practice activities keep their existing hint records. Watching a tutorial is not a learner attempt, a mastery result, or proof of unaided understanding.

A **new arithmetic round** is created after its tutorial closes, so its bonus window starts then. Reopening the tutorial during an existing round, or resuming that round later, does not pause or reset its server-owned deadline. The bonus window never stops play or removes earned points.
